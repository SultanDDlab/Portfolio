The project

Giving physical form to an animated world.

For this project, I created a new sound-effects track for the short animated film Alma. I chose to work primarily with Foley, challenging myself to build the film’s physical world from ordinary household objects.

Every performed effect was developed through experimentation, recording and close synchronization with the image. The ambient wind and music were not created by me.

Foley materials

01

Footsteps in snow

A cup filled with oatmeal was handled rhythmically to create the compressed texture of footsteps in snow.

02

Bicycle pedals

A wine opener supplied the small metallic rattles and mechanical movement of the toy bicycle.

03

Squeaking door

A toilet lid became the source for the door’s strained and uneasy creak.

Reflection

Familiar objects can lose their original identity when they are recorded, shaped and placed against an image.

It was an enjoyable learning process that strengthened my understanding of performance, timing and material texture. Working with household utensils encouraged closer listening and more inventive problem-solving.
