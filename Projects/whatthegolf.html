The project

This project was created in 2022 as an entrance-exam submission for an educational programme.

I composed the music and selected, arranged and synchronized the sound effects for a trailer from WHAT THE GOLF?.

The work was produced using GarageBand and edited to picture in iMovie.

Sound approach

01

Original music

I composed the music to support the trailer’s pacing, movement and playful visual character.

02

Sound-effects edit

I selected and assembled sound effects, then synchronized them with the actions and transitions in the trailer.
