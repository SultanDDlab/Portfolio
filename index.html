About

Atmosphere before excess.

I hear music everywhere, so I chose to dedicate my life to capturing it.
I create sonic experiences for film, animation and visual media. Some remain quietly in the background. Others step forward and invite interaction. I use sound to understand what lies beneath an image, shaping melancholy, distance and emotional depth into a shared sonic language.

Available for collaborations, student productions and selected freelance projects.
